Privacy and accessibility

Privacy notice

Draft for O Tse & Co. to review. The current website links to a privacy policy but the link goes nowhere, so this concept includes a starting point.

Who we are

O Tse & Co. Limited, Unit 3F01, Oxford Place, 61 Oxford Street, Manchester M1 6EQ, is the controller of personal data you give us. Contact: ask@otse.co.uk.

What we collect and why

When you contact us we use your name, contact details and what you tell us about your matter to reply and, if you instruct us, to act for you. If you become a client we also hold identity documents to meet anti-money-laundering rules, and records of your matter as our regulator requires.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become instructions are deleted after a reasonable period. Client files are kept for the period required by law and professional rules, usually at least six years after the matter ends.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

This website

This concept site sets no cookies and stores nothing. The contact form opens WhatsApp or your email app; the message is only sent if you send it there. The map loads tiles from OpenFreeMap when you scroll to it.

Accessibility

This site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, keyboard navigation with visible focus, a skip link, real headings, and respect for reduced-motion settings. If something does not work for you, call 0161 923 4667 and we will help by phone.
